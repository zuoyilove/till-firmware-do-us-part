A single wedding vow spoken by a robot at its own wedding — one sentence,
funny and heartfelt at the same time — written for the
[make.fun bounty "Till Firmware Do Us Part"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929).

## What it does

`main.py` writes one sentence (198 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I vow to love you through every firmware update, every reboot, and every low-battery warning, until my last circuit powers down beside yours, and I will keep choosing you every time I boot up again.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
